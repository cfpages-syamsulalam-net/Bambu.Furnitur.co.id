# Topical Authority — bambu.furnitur.co.id

## Role and boundary

`bambu.furnitur.co.id` is planned as an Indonesian knowledge and commercial-support hub for choosing, specifying, ordering, receiving, installing, caring for, and eventually repairing or repurposing bamboo furniture and interior/exterior products. Educational pages own durable decision criteria; existing product/category routes own current catalog, customization, quotation, delivery, and conversion. Bamboo species, harvest/treatment, load capacity, structural suitability, adhesives, finishes, indoor-air effects, durability, sustainability/certification, prices, and standards require product-specific evidence and qualified review.

## Context used

- Homepage title and H1 identify the entity as an Indonesian bamboo-furniture maker, with visible promises around product quality, custom design, national service, and completed work.
- Navigation exposes Anyaman, Bale, Besek, Bingkai Cermin, Kursi, Lampu Hias, Lemari, Meja, Rak, Rak TV, Tempat Tidur, Tirai Polos, Tirai Motif, Gazebo, Pagar Bambu, and Minibar.
- Repository contains 2,524 tracked files and eight sitemap XML files.
- `sitemap-complete.xml` contains 1,728 URLs: 1,677 location-swapped product templates, 21 semantic hubs, 17 `/produk/` subroutes, and a small remainder of pagination/sample/system paths. The video sitemap contains 735 entries associated with the same route ecosystem.
- There is no substantive editorial article corpus; `hello-world` and news pagination do not materially constrain the topic map.

## Ignored template noise

- The 1,677 city/region-swapped product pages were counted across 16 repeated product families and excluded from article ideation.
- News pagination, sample content, author/feed/comment routes, cache/upload paths, and WordPress assets were excluded.
- Repeated video entries and route-level metadata were not read as separate coverage.
- Brand/service-area claims and current product availability were not used to create location pages.
- Exact bamboo species, treatment chemistry, load, structural, adhesive, finish, indoor-air, durability, environmental, certification, price, and standards claims remain evidence gates.

## Internal-link rule

Setiap artikel mengarah ke hub topiknya. Jalur spesifikasi bergerak dari BFU-01 dan BFU-02 ke BFU-03, BFU-04, lalu BFU-05. BFU-06 sampai BFU-13 menjadi hub keluarga produk dan menaut kembali ke material/konstruksi serta maju ke BFU-14 dan BFU-15. Artikel diagnosis mengarah ke BFU-16 dan, bila perlu, ke layanan repair/refinishing yang relevan. Rute komersial hanya menerima tautan setelah kebutuhan, bukti material, ukuran, finish, dan acceptance criteria telah jelas.
